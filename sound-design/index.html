Why finish still resolves, even without melody. Click→Double‑Click has almost no pitch, so it can't resolve harmonically the way Open→Resolve does. Instead resolution comes from the second hit itself: same click voice, slightly brighter/higher, arriving right after the first — the ear reads "click, click‑!" as one completed gesture the same way it reads a finished chord, just without any melody to get fatiguing across hundreds of plays a day.

About the "Apple Dictation" reference. I don't have access to macOS's actual dictation sound file — it's a proprietary asset I can't extract or copy, so what's below is a synthesized approximation built from the general, well‑known character of that sound (two quick plain notes up, mirrored down on stop), not a byte‑for‑byte match. Treat it as a rough shape reference, not ground truth. For the real thing, turn on Dictation on your own Mac (System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation) and trigger it live — that's the only fully accurate comparison.

Using the exported files in the app. Downloads are 16‑bit PCM WAV. For lowest playback latency with NSSound/AVAudioPlayer, convert to CAF:
