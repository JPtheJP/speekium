The idea

It never cuts the sentence.

Chunked transcription slices audio into pieces, and the cuts land mid-word — exactly where a mixed sentence breaks. Speekium re-reads the whole utterance instead, several times a second.

Chunked · the cut lands mid-word

Whole · read again from the start

Bring the right model

Streaming is a behavior, not a model requirement.

Use another supported transcription model or a compatible fine-tuned checkpoint. Even when the model only returns a finished transcript, Speekium repeatedly re-reads the growing utterance and settles the stable words into a live stream.
